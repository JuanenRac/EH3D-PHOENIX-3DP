# Roadmap

Nine steps from the base that exists today to a complete program. Each step ends with its tests and its documentation up to date, and nothing is claimed about a real printer without evidence from a real printer. The state of each step is told honestly: nothing is green until it has run on a machine.

| Step | What it is | State |
|---|---|---|
| H0 | The frame and the base: the workspace, the CI, the units, the mesh and its checks, the readers and writers of the formats, the catalog of profiles, the G-code writers with limits that refuse, the driver trait and the Moonraker and OctoPrint drivers against simulators, the thin link to the server, the command line and the base of the window | done, with simulators only |
| H1 | Mesh and geometry: more operations on the mesh, repair of what can be repaired, and a 3D viewer of its own (wgpu) with the plate and the orbit | begun: the model of the viewer (orbit camera with perspective, picking, selection box, range of layers, lines of a plan) is written and tested without a screen, and the drawing with wgpu is written behind a feature and never built; the cut of a mesh by planes exists, in the slicer |
| H2 | A minimal slicer: slices, regions, classic perimeters, rectilinear and grid infill, top and bottom layers, G-code for Marlin and Klipper, an estimate of the time, a command `slice`, and a calibration cube checked against the geometry | done, with simulators only: slices, perimeters, rectilinear, grid and gyroid infill, solid skins, bridges, plain supports, seams, G-code for the four dialects, an estimate of the time with acceleration, the command `slice`, properties checked on shapes from a fixed seed; the calibration cube printed on a machine is still to do |
| H3 | Profiles and the base interface: the editor of profiles with inheritance (printer, filament, process), the list of objects, plates, and the sync of profiles with the server | begun: a catalog of 120 printer models that says where the limits of each come from, twelve filaments, seven resins and their check against a printer and a nozzle, and the edit of a profile that says what changed and tells a limit raised past the maker's; the lists of printers and filaments have filters in the window; the editor and the sync are not started |
| H4 | Quality: variable width of the lines, the position of the seam, ironing, fuzzy skin, bridges and overhangs, retraction and z-hop, pressure advance, brim, skirt and raft, vase mode, variable layer height, arc fitting | begun: a simple variable width of the perimeters, the position of the seam, bridges, retraction with z-hop and a fan that climbs by layers; skirt, brim and raft, slower short layers, retraction that follows the travel and a vase mode; the rest is not started |
| H5 | Supports and advanced infill: normal and tree supports, painting of supports and seams, gyroid, honeycomb, cubic, lightning and adaptive infill, and a viewer of the G-code by layers with its types and speeds | begun: plain supports, grid and gyroid infill; the lines of a plan by layers with colours for each kind, and the travel moves, are data in the viewer model; the rest is not started |
| H6 | Multi-material: purge tower and purge map, grouping by nozzle, toolchangers and dual extruders, painting of colours, reading 3MF with plates, and writing the G-code package that the Bambu printers take | begun: the objects, plates and settings of the 3MF projects of other programs are read (read only) |
| H7 | Connectivity and the server: network transports for Moonraker and OctoPrint, PrusaLink and Duet, the queue in the server, the state in real time, the camera, the checks before printing, the reports; the Bambu LAN link as an experimental option | begun: the protocol of the Bambu link (MQTT packets, FTPS commands, the pinned certificate) as bytes tried against a broker in memory, with no TLS to open it; a plain HTTP transport for Moonraker and OctoPrint, and a client of the `printer3d` plugin of the server over the common client of the suite, with the rules of the queue (no repeated job, a longest line), tried against a socket of the same computer and a server in memory; the WebSocket push, PrusaLink, Duet, the camera and Bambu LAN are still to do |
| H8 | Guided calibrations, a simulator or twin, profiles that have evidence from real machines, and the simulation of collisions | not started |
| H9 | Polish: configurable shortcuts, themes, all eight languages complete (the names of the states are translated now), mobile and touch-screen reading of results, the documentation, the screenshots and a signed package | not started |

## What is decided

* The EH3D server and its clients are the centre: the printers, the queue and the permissions live in the server, and this program is one more client. The stand-alone mode that talks straight to a printer is optional and off by default.
* FDM and resin (mSLA) printers are both in the catalog from the start; the drivers for resin printers come later.
* Moonraker/Klipper and OctoPrint come first, with simulators; Bambu LAN is experimental; the cloud of Bambu is optional and off.
* The queue never starts a job by itself unless the policy of the server says so, and the default is off.
* The temperature limits are the ones that the maker states for each model, kept in a profile that the owner can edit.
* Slicing may also run in the server, as an option.
* Projects: read the packages of other programs as models, write the own 3MF and, later, a 3MF with G-code that the printers that need one accept.
* The viewer is written for this program (wgpu); the shortcuts of the family are the default, with a scheme of its own.
* The name carries a notice about the marks of third parties, and nothing is copied from any other slicer: the program is written from public protocols, manuals and facts.
